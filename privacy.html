Privacy Policy for PDF Toolkit

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the PDF Toolkit application (the "App").

1. File Processing (100% Local)

PDF Toolkit is designed with privacy as a top priority. All PDF merging and processing happens entirely on your device using your browser's memory. We do not upload, store, view, or transmit your PDF files to any external servers. Once you close the app or refresh the page, your files are completely removed from memory.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information directly. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use the App.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Adsterra)

To keep this app free, we display advertisements provided by our advertising partner, Adsterra. These third-party ad networks may use cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

Adsterra may collect non-personal data such as your device type, browser type, IP address, and general usage data to serve relevant ads. You can learn more about how Adsterra handles data by visiting their Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
